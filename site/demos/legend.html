Legend

Three layers show three ways to define legend entries. The legend panel follows the layers currently visible on the map.

Try it: switch layers off and on to see their legend entries disappear and return. Layers outside their zoom range are also omitted.

Three sources, one panel

  • From the service: legend: "auto" on a WMS layer builds a GetLegendGraphic request
  • From the style: vector layers with literal colours produce swatches automatically
  • By hand: an array of entries with label, colour and shape (square, line, circle)

Set legend: false to hide a layer from the legend. A URL string loads a custom legend image, for example one hosted alongside your configuration.

Legends for data-driven styles. A style may vary its colour by attribute or its width by zoom. map0 derives swatches from constant colours only. For styles based on expressions, define the legend entries yourself.
