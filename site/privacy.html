Privacy policy

map0.net is a static website. It sets no cookies, has no accounts and no forms, and its analytics are cookieless and anonymous. This page explains where personal data still comes into play, and why.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies. The only things kept in your browser are your own choices — language and light/dark theme — stored locally (localStorage) and never transmitted.
  • No accounts, logins, forms or newsletters.
  • No advertising and no cross-site tracking.
  • No profiles of individual visitors. Analytics are aggregated and anonymous.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.). To deliver a page, the web server necessarily processes the technical data of each request, such as the IP address, the requested URL, the time and the browser identification, and may retain it briefly in server logs for security and operations. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the site reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Microsoft processes this data on our behalf under its data protection addendum.

Web analytics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, self-hosted on Spatial Focus infrastructure, so no analytics data leaves our own systems. Umami sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and does not track individual visitors. It only produces aggregated, anonymous statistics such as page views and referrers. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Analytics run only on the deployed site, never in local development builds.

Map data from third-party services

The live demos are real maps. When one loads, your browser fetches basemaps and geodata directly from the servers of third parties, and those requests carry your IP address and browser identification, like any web request. The services in use:

  • basemap.at and City of Vienna geodata (mapsneu.wien.gv.at, data.wien.gv.at, wien.gv.at), operated by the City of Vienna and the Austrian basemap.at cooperation.
  • MapLibre demo tiles (demotiles.maplibre.org), operated by the MapLibre project and used by the globe demo.
  • ICGC basemaps and Geomatico sample rasters (geoserveis.icgc.cat, labs.geomatico.es), operated by the Institut Cartogràfic i Geològic de Catalunya and by Geomatico, used by the Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF demo.

One demo raster — the sealed-surfaces dataset — is not fetched from its publisher but from our own object storage (map0.s3.fr-par.scw.cloud) at Scaleway SAS in France, because the file has to be converted before a browser can read it. Scaleway stores it and delivers it on our behalf as a processor, in the EU; the request carries your IP address like any other. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in demonstrating map0 with real data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

These operators act as independent controllers of the requests they receive; their own privacy policies apply. The maps are the purpose of this site, so they load with the page. If you prefer not to contact these services, do not open the demo pages.
